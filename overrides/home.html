Keep EdgeTX moving

Every contribution makes a difference.

EdgeTX grows through the time, ideas and contributions of its community. Help sustain the project in a way that works for you.

Make a contribution

Open Collective

Keeping the project running takes infrastructure, hosting and build services. Your financial support helps cover these costs. We keep our finances fully transparent on Open Collective, where anyone can see contributions and how funds are spent.

Donate now

Wear your support

Wear your support with EdgeTX clothing and accessories. Once our merchandise shop launches, a portion of every purchase will go towards the project’s budget, helping keep EdgeTX moving.

Bring your skills

Help us put new releases to the test. Testing across different radios and setups is one of the most valuable ways to contribute. Try upcoming features, report what you find and help us catch issues before release. You don’t have to be a developer: documentation, translations, design and helping other users matter too. Whether you have an hour to spare or want to get involved regularly, your experience makes a difference.
