# Community

Meet other RC enthusiasts, ask questions, exchange ideas and share what you build. Whether you are setting up your first radio or have years of experience, there is a place for you in the EdgeTX community.

## Connect and share

- [Discord](https://discord.gg/wF9wUKnZ6H) — join conversations, get advice and share your setups.
- [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/groups/edgetx) — connect with other users.
- [RC Groups](https://www.rcgroups.com/forums/showthread.php?3916381-Official-EdgeTX-Discussion-Thread) — take part in the EdgeTX discussion thread.

For help with a specific issue, see [Support](support.md). Please follow the [Community Guidelines](https://github.com/EdgeTX/edgetx.github.io/wiki/Community-Guidlines) when participating.

## News and events

Follow [project news](news.md) and meet the community at EdgeTX Fest. Explore the [2026](fest2026.md), [2025](fest2025.md), [2024](fest2024.md) and [2023](fest2023.md) editions.

## Community creations

Explore [Themes](https://edgetx.org/themes/) and [Lua scripts & widgets](https://edgetx.org/lua-scripts/) created by users, or take a look at the [developer contests](devcontest.md).

## Get involved

You can help through code, testing, documentation and more. Visit [Get involved](recruiting.md) to find a way to contribute.
