# About EdgeTX

## What is EdgeTX?

EdgeTX is open-source firmware for RC transmitters, designed for maximum flexibility.

- **Configure your models your way.** A comprehensive set of tools lets you configure virtually any RC model, with multiple ways to approach each setup.
- **Choose your hardware.** Support for a wide range of transmitters from multiple manufacturers. Explore the [supported radios](supportedradios.md).
- **Connect your telemetry.** Compatibility with a broad range of telemetry protocols.
- **Make it your own.** Customize your radio visually with themes and extend its functionality with Lua scripts and widgets.
- **Explore and contribute.** Openly available source code lets anyone understand how EdgeTX works, contribute improvements and help shape its development.

## Our story

EdgeTX began on 6 May 2021 as a fork of OpenTX, with the aim of creating a more optimized, touch-friendly user interface. It has continued to develop through contributions from the RC community, including improvements to Lua scripting, hardware support and the tools used alongside the firmware.

The source code is available in the [EdgeTX GitHub organization](https://github.com/EdgeTX).

## How the project is organized

EdgeTX is a community project governed by a Project Steering Committee (PSC). The Development Team maintains and develops the software, alongside contributors who help with code, testing, documentation, translations and user support.

The [Project Bylaws](bylaws.md) define how the project operates, including responsibilities, elections, decision-making and team membership.

Meet the people behind the project on the [Team page](team.md).
